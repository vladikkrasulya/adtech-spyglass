before transition; local and public health/smoke passed; the container read back healthy with
`restart: always`, zero restarts, and rollback tag `rollback-pre-f6bead9`. Sentry remained
`ready:false`, which is a documented non-gate and does not prove or disprove upstream delivery.

The dialect labeller was built before its gate, and further from it than 004 was. It shipped on
2026-08-18 against an accepted ADR that had considered and rejected exactly that design, with no
feature package, no decision record, no privacy-contract section and no release note — and it was
unreachable in production the entire time, so nothing exercised it. On 2026-08-20 the regression
test enforcing that ADR blocked the repair, was relaxed on the mistaken reading that it guarded a
removed bridge, and three changes were deployed before any of this was written down. The gate would
have produced ADR-012 first, and ADR-012 is what makes the feature defensible rather than merely
present. The package and the ADR both carry the true order rather than a date implying otherwise.

The silent-failure work was built before its gate. Thirty-one commits landed on
`feat/url-search-feed` while this file said product features were not scheduled, and the
assessment and feature package were written afterwards. Both records say so rather than carrying a
date that implies otherwise. The gate would have caught that the work was four bounded problems
rather than one, and that three of the four produced modules with no consumer — corrected before
the record was written, at the cost of a full cycle.

The public-control-language repair likewise began from a direct visual audit while the machine-local
feature pointer still named completed feature 005. The release audit stopped the commit, created 006,
reconciled its requirements and evidence, and separated the unrelated compatibility-registry intake.
The package records that order explicitly; it is recovery of traceability, not a claim that planning
preceded the already completed browser work.

The dialect direction is **paused** as of 2026-08-22, by the owner's decision, from a closed state
rather than an abandoned one: 008 is complete, merged and deployed, the corpus stands at 1233 rules
with every mutation carrying provenance, and the lab containers are stopped with their image and
data retained. What remains open is named in the 008 row above — seven disposition disagreements in
the adjudication follow-up queue, twelve rules the end-to-end oracle cannot isolate, and route
relevance parked until a record-bearing source exists. Resuming starts from that row and from the
audit bundle beside the corpus; nothing resumable lives only in a conversation.
